# SLINT Tech

**Selfless Leadership and Innovation for a New Tomorrow**

A registered nonprofit in Ghana equipping young people with technical skills, mentorship, and
values-driven leadership — so they can build a prosperous, just society.

## Our Vision

To empower a new generation of selfless, innovative leaders in Ghana, united in their commitment
to transform society through technology, integrity, and a deep sense of purpose. We envision a
future where young minds are equipped to break barriers, challenge the status quo, and lead with
courage — using their skills to create a prosperous and just society that honors God, serves the
community, and uplifts our nation.

## Our Mission

To create a community of young people passionate about learning and building together. Through
hands-on coding education, mentorship, and values-driven leadership training, SLINT Tech equips
youth with technical skills, a growth-oriented mindset, and a commitment to ethical innovation.

## Our Values

| Value | What it means here |
| --- | --- |
| **Integrity** | We uphold honesty, transparency, and ethical practice in everything we build. |
| **Unity** | We collaborate, we don't divide. Every member should feel supported and connected. |
| **Selflessness** | We contribute without personal gain, helping each other and advancing the mission. |
| **Innovation** | We encourage creativity and problem-solving that makes real impact. |

## What We Do

- **Tech education** — hands-on coding, design, and digital-skills training through structured
  weekly learning paths.
- **Career cells** — members join a cell matched to their career path and learn, build, and ship
  together under a Cell Leader.
- **Leadership development** — programs grounded in selfless leadership and ethical innovation.
- **Community impact** — outreach projects, tech-for-good solutions, and open-source work.
- **Partnerships** — learning platforms and recruiters who help members grow and get hired.

## Career Cells

`Software Engineering` · `Frontend Development` · `Backend Development` · `Cybersecurity`
`Cloud Computing` · `Data Analysis` · `Data Science` · `AI & Machine Learning`

Each cell runs weekly projects, lesson tracks, and project showcases, with monthly and annual
awards for outstanding work.
